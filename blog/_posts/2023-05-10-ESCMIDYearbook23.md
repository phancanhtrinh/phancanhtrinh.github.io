The *ESCMID Research Yearbook 2022–23* features me in its **Awards & Grants – Research Grants** section on **printed page 110 (PDF page 112)**. My profile highlights my project at the Medical University of Vienna, **“Rom1 as a fungal sensing factor during skin colonization of the human fungal pathogen Candida auris,”** alongside my reflections on science and discovery.

I am grateful to the European Society of Clinical Microbiology and Infectious Diseases for supporting my research through an **ESCMID Research Grant in 2023**.

To preserve the feature if the original link changes in the future, a copy of the complete yearbook is archived on this website and embedded below. The viewer opens at the lower part of printed page 110, where my portrait and profile appear.
