The September 2026 issue of *MedUnique people* features me on page 5 among MedUni Wien researchers recognized for outstanding scientific achievements. The feature highlights the **Austrian Microbiology Prize 2026** and summarizes our *Nature Microbiology* discovery that *Candida auris* uses a CO₂-based metabolic strategy to survive on skin and better tolerate antifungal therapies.

To preserve the feature if the original magazine link changes in the future, a copy of the complete issue is archived on this website and embedded below.
