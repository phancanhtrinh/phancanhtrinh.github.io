# Summary

*Candida auris* (*Candidozyma auris*) is an urgent global health threat because it persists on skin, spreads efficiently in healthcare settings, and frequently resists antifungal treatment. These traits have driven outbreaks worldwide and made life-threatening invasive infections increasingly difficult to manage.

This review examines the molecular basis of multidrug resistance in *C. auris* and related emerging fungal pathogens. It synthesizes current knowledge of clinical resistance mechanisms and highlights recent progress in antifungal drug discovery and therapeutic strategies that may improve clinical management.
